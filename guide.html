Questions?

How long does it take to set up?
About 5 minutes. Pay $1, copy the code, replace your merchant ID, and paste it on your website.
Do I need coding knowledge?
No. The code is ready to copy-paste. You only replace your merchant ID and paste it on your site. You can also restyle the button if you want.
Can I customize the Pay Now button?
Yes. Change the label, colors, size, or shape. Do not change the checkout link except for your merchant ID, and keep the Powered by NoWalletConnect line.
What are the fees?
$1 setup when you sign up, plus $1/month for maintenance. No transaction fees. You keep 100% of every payment.
What does the $1/month cover?
Server maintenance, payment processing, dashboard hosting, and support for your account.
How do customers pay?
They click Pay Now, enter an amount, choose Polygon or Solana, and send USDT to the payment address. Payment is verified automatically.
Can I cancel my account?
Yes, anytime. No long-term contract and no cancellation fee.
Where does the money go?
Directly to your wallet. You control the funds. No middleman and no extra cut from us.
